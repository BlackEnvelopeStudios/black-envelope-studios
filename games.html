The Veiled Invitation

The Veiled Invitation is a semi-cooperative horror tabletop game that combines RPG elements with forced cooperation, hidden motives, and full-blown backstabbing.

Players must work together while deciding how much they can afford to trust one another. Alliances offer an advantage, but another player’s help may come with intentions of their own. Every decision can shift the balance between cooperation and betrayal.

The experience is built around the people at the table: the accusations, uneasy bargains, unexpected loyalties, and ruthless decisions that everyone remembers long after the game ends. Horror sets the atmosphere. The players give each other reasons to be afraid.

In development since 2018, The Veiled Invitation is Black Envelope Studios’ first game.
