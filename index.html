The studio

Every table deserves a secret.

We design games where the rules create pressure and the players create the story.

Black Envelope Studios is an independent board game studio focused on immersive, story-first experiences. Our games are built around tension, suspicion, shifting alliances, and the occasional beautifully terrible decision.

The studio's first project is The Veiled Invitation, currently in development.
